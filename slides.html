HCHIS
Host-City Stadium Heat Intelligence System

A real-data instrument for all 11 FIFA World Cup 2026 US stadiums — heat risk, parking capacity, and now, live coverage of their real ongoing 2026 NFL season.

01 The problem

World Cup 2026 puts millions of fans in some of the hottest stadiums in the country — and every existing heat tool answers the wrong question.

City-wide heat indices. Generic weather widgets. One WBGT number pinned to a stadium's coordinates. None of them tell a venue operator which of 40 real parking lots to open first, or how hot the walk from the car actually is.

02 Why nothing like this exists

Every tool stops at the parking lot fence.

09 The discipline underneath it

Every number carries its own receipt.
